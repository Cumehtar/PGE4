Each year, [[Solitude]] steadily amasses more and more visitors from abroad, here to see how life truly was in the age of the Septims. Quiet and sparsely populated compared to the trade cities of the southern seas, Solitude’s cobbled streets and fresh sea breezes provide a welcome respite from the hustle and bustle of modernity.

Solitude’s port, reopened in recent years, sees a slow but steady flow of sea trade. The [[East Empire Company]] has reopened its warehouse at the docks, graciously providing a steady - but limited! - supply of southern goods and delicacies to the people of Solitude, all housed in the most modern of facilities.

Many of the city’s mansions have been purchased by foreign merchants and visitors, which can only be a good thing for the prosperity of the city. These once-grand estates, worn down by decades if not centuries of neglect and disrepair, are being brought back to their former glories - with modern amenities and sensibilities, of course - heralding a return to Solitude’s past and hopeful future as a cosmopolitan trade city, jewel of the North.

The city’s main square is decorated with a dual statue of the realm’s founding queens, Elisif and Gwynienne, greeting visitors in stone as they once did in the flesh before the tragedies of Plague and War. Such statues are common across the city - commissioned by kings and queens of the past, they commemorate the noble rulers as they were in life, wise and solemn. Today, work has begun on a statue depicting the current king, his Highness Gaspard Clovis Bjornsson Lariat-Septim de Haafingar II, King of [[Greater Wrothgaria and Karth]], Earl of Haafingar, Dragon-Wolf of Solitude, True Heir of Septims, Blessed of Akatosh. Not content with a simple traditional work of stone, [[King Gaspard]] has hired skilled artisans from the Potentate to capture his visage, the marble entwined with enchantments against erosion and decay.

The Blue Palace is the dwelling of King Gaspard and his retinue. Traditionally, the monarch of the realm would only hold court here in the coldest months of winter, spending the rest of their time wandering across the land, imposing on the hospitality of their vassals. His highness has, by and large, ended this tradition in his reign, instead choosing to remain in Solitude year-round - a change that the lords and ladies of the land no doubt appreciate. Visitors to Solitude thus have constant access to the King, should they wish an appointment to be made, to speak of trade and legislation.

The great Seminary of the Divines and Cathedral of the Eight occupies Castle Dour as well as many of the neighboring buildings of the city. The Seminary is the Kingdom’s prime learning institution of not only theology, but applied magic as well. The Temple’s rigid traditionalism has kept the Kingdom’s practices of organized magic the most conservative in Tamriel, bound even more to the outdated paradigm of Schools than even that of the mad barbarian engineers of the College of [[Winterhold]]. Most of the Kingdom’s formally educated practitioners one might meet would have been educated here as the lay members of the Temple, if not priests - calling to Mara and Stendarr for Restoration, Dibella for Illusion, Arkay for Mysticism, Julianos for Conjuration, Zenithar for Alteration, and Kynareth and Akatosh for Destruction. The Seminary and Cathedral act as the seat of the Patriarch of Akatosh, the ostensible head of the Cult in the Kingdom - a controversial figure, to be sure, so strident in his opposition to what he deems “neo-Alessian” theologies that he has been dubbed the “Archbishop of Solitude”. Nonetheless, the Patriarch has been an instrumental position in the history of the Kingdom, being responsible for the investiture of each new monarch upon their ascension to the throne.

Guided tours of Solitude are available for visitors, sponsored and organized by the East Empire Company and the Embassy of the Potentate in Greater Wrothgaria and Karth.
