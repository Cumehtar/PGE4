The Kings and Queens of Wrothgaria lay claim to two lineages to legitimize their rule and aspirations to true Imperial heritage: that of the founding Queens, Elisif of [[Solitude]] and Gwynienne of [[Northpoint]], and that of Uriel Lariat and his son Andorak Septim, who, after the Ruby Throne passed to Cephorus, was granted the title of King of [[Shornhelm]].

Tracing the history of the royal line is an arduous task. Elisif and Gwynienne, while attested to as the founding queens, have scant few records of their pasts and origins remaining - or even the date of their marriage.1 Elisif’s birthplace is indeed unknown - some apocryphal records suggest that she was the youngest daughter of [[Bruma]]n nobility, while others place her as the scion of Bretonordic High Rock royals, and others yet paint her as a Skyrim native. So too is Gwynienne’s history unclear - while many place her as having descent from Shornhelm and thereby the Lariat line, all that can be said for certain is that she was queen of Northpoint.2

Further complicating attempts at accurate genealogy is the lack of direct heirs to the queens. Neither queen had children of their own - barring ever-present rumors of a secret child of Elisif and Torygg, her first husband - and records of who was designated their direct heir are missing from the remaining writings of the [[Deepwoods Period of Solitude]]. What remains suggests a mutual adoption of and intermarriage between the nieces and nephews of the queens3, producing a single, albeit large and immediately fractured, royal family and establishing a now oft-followed precedent of adoption into and between lines.

The fracture of the line of the Queens has reared its head many times throughout the Kingdom’s history. Competing claims and unclear lineages, combined with the devolution of power from the Crown to local nobility, have led to no shortage of wars of succession. While Solitude has remained preeminent in the Kingdom, Northpoint and even Shornhelm have at times been the seats of rival kings and queens. These wars have as often been resolved peacefully as violently, following the precedent of the founders via mutual adoption and intermarriage.

**Editor's Note:**
_1. While commonly accepted historiography, especially by scholars in the Kingdom, dates the marriage to 4e204, proclamations co-signed by both Queens do not appear until 4e207, beginning with the first writs of taxation for the thanes, barons, and echevins of what would become the Kingdom. Journals from court stewards in Solitude point to an alliance yet not outright marriage between the Queens beginning in 4e204, created out of necessity to stabilize an increasingly post-Imperial north._

_2. The first claims to legitimacy via Septim descent appear in 4e265, in a secession dispute between [[Lysane of Northpoint]] and [[Erik of Solitude]]. Lysane claimed a higher right to the throne from her alleged closer relation to the Lariat line. Resolved by a tense yet ultimately fruitful marriage between the parties, the claim of Septim ancestry has since been a staple of Wrothgarian nobility, even coming to be applied retroactively._

_3. Three marriages between the nieces and nephews present at the hillfort during the Deepwood Period are recorded, with allusions to at least one more. The earliest surviving records point to these unions receiving rulership of Northpoint, Solitude, and Dragon Bridge._
