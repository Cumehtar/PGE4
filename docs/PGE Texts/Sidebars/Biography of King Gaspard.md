Wrothgaria’s current king traces his humble origins to the town of Dragon Bridge, a holding his branch of the royal line has held since the earliest days of the Kingdom. Gaspard was born as the only child of his parents, and schooled from a young age in the intricacies of governance and trade - a cosmopolitan education, aided by [[Dragon Bridge]]’s position on the [[Legion Road]], as many of his tutors were of foreign, educated persuasion. Gaspard was thus exposed from his earliest years to the benefits of connection to the wider world, speaking and dining with merchants, soldiers, and diplomats who passed through his family’s court.

Tragedy struck Gaspard not once, not twice, but thrice before he came of age. His parents took ill when he was but ten, confining them to their beds, and ultimately their graves, leaving governance of Dragon Bridge to the young lord. Three years later, his aunt and Queen of the Realm, Lady Tiffany Lariat, was murdered in her sleep one cold winter night, a vile act blamed upon Torrygian Rebels, who deny the legitimacy of the royal line even two centuries after Skyrim’s Civil War and dissolution. Childless, Lady Tiffany’s closest relative and heir was thus the young Gaspard, who gravely traveled to Solitude for his investiture, receiving the weight of crown and authority at the tender age of thirteen. King Gaspard’s first act as ruler was to dismiss his aunt’s bodyguards and heralds, the Wolf’s Eyes (readers may recognize this _Lupus Oculatus_ as a relative of the Potentate’s own _Penitus Oculatus_) and replace them with his own guard, drawn and employed from the soldiers and traders he had met as lord of Dragon Bridge, free from any malign domestic insurrection.

In the years since, King Gaspard has ruled the Kingdom wisely and justly. The royal court has taken up near-permanent residence at the Blue Palace, restoring what was once but a temporary home for the monarchs to its rightful place as center of governance and diplomacy. Foreign diplomats, envoys, and merchants have been welcomed with open arms, and investment has flowed, filling the coffers of the Kingdom, of Solitude, and of the people alike. A revival of the arts and culture, spearheaded by foreign artists and artisans employed by the royal court, has beautified Solitude as never before. Under his benevolent eye, thegn-barons along the Legion Road have imported the most modern of Potentate soul-machines, allowing for the production of valuable alchemical ingredients at a scale never before seen, enriching the land further.

The King graciously entertains visitors, diplomats, and merchants at his court in Solitude as well as his storied childhood home of Dragon Bridge. Inquire at the nearest embassy or at the Blue Palace if you wish to make an appointment - vetting by the _Lupus Oculatus_ will be required.
