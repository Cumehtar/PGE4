_Capable. Resourceful. Hard folk, but you have to be, to survive in the wilds. Don't let anyone tell you otherwise, the Legions are the true heirs to the Empire. And one day we'll show those fools in [[Cheydinhal]] so._

Of course the remains of the Legions turned to banditry during the Days of Long Wolves. Who didn’t? More important is whether they remained that way, and as I see it, those who call themselves the Legion today turned away from banditry. Are they true heirs to the Legions, though? Who can say?
