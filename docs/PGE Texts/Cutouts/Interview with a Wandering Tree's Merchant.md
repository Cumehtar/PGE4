**History and legend alike speak of the great “Walking City” of [[Falinesti]]. A living arborpolis considered sacred to all devotees of Y’ffre, it rooted itself permanently in the waning days of the Third Era. But the Roaming Tree reappeared decades ago, amazing the inhabitants of the southwestern forests of Tamriel. To learn more about this miraculous marvel, I have decided to speak with someone living within its boughs. Your correspondent with the Courier Guild spoke with Rakalai, an Imga who sells charms and potions in her shop** _**The Top Branch**_.

_Many thanks for agreeing to this interview. Let me start by asking: what inspired you to set up shop in such an unusual location? The branch of a walking tree?_

RALAKAI: My ancestors have lived in her branches for generations. We adapt to the age, when need be, yet keep the old traditions alive. Take for sample this Special Hoarvor Elixir, which is made from dried hoarvor bloat sacs and imported redwort petals, as well as a secret ingredient of my own, sure to cure most poisons you will encounter in the southern jungles. A potion like that, with ingredients both foreign and local, is easier to make here because Falinesti is always moving, so trade must always flow with it.

_Indeed, I noticed many merchants from abroad as I entered the city. A significant number of them seemed to be from [[Anequina]], even though the city is currently rooted in Sunnamora._

RALAKAI: Yes, the cat-folk of Ne-Quin’al must trade their goods here and now while they can. Soon, Falinesti will move south for the long Winter Year in Pa’alatiin. The southern cat-folk will become the new governors of the Great Tree. They forbid all commerce from the northern Mane’s land, which they say could be a “corrupting influence.” I stay out of all that political nonsense, though. If you like Khajiiti goods, I have a sphinx-charm here which resembles Agamanus. It is said to ward off the dark spirits, at least until the magicks run out. 

_Ah, yes. By the way, I’ve noticed there are other merchants in the boughs. Taverns, leatherworkers, outfitters, a bookseller… And even a Bank of Z’en branch._

RALAKAI: Yes, everyone does well enough in trade with the movement of the Great Tree. And the Bank provides loans when need be. They also aid with currency changes. If you are lacking for currency, I take barter as well, so you know. 

_Very good. You mentioned earlier the change in government that occurs roughly every year in this city due to its movement. This would sound perplexing to many readers back home, who are used to relatively stable governments. So, could you describe how this works?_

RALAKAI: Well, the current governors, that’s the… what is the word they always use? Adon. The adon and the other fancy Ayleid lords strut around the Great Tree right now, making sure we all follow their proper laws and regulations while she rests in Sunnamora. But soon they will move into their estates in the "waiting town.”

_The small city surrounding Falinesti. I passed through it to get here._

RALAKAI: Yes. And then she moves south, to Pa’alatiin, as I spoke of earlier. There is a “waiting town” there, too. With its own guards and governors. And another at the Tree's next resting spot, where many Green Prophets wait; and the next spot, where the Camorans wait. Each time we must adjust to meet the trade laws of the local governors, for about a year as you say. Always staying ready to adapt. Such is life with the Great Walking Tree. If _you_ need to go for a long walk anytime soon, I have a special beetle tonic which soothes leg pains.

_But what about during the transitional periods when the Great Tree is walking? Who governs the city then?_

RALAKAI: We take care of ourselves. The Greenwardens do well enough to keep the peace.

_Interesting. Now, there are many who wonder why Falinesti started moving again just a few decades ago. I heard one Ayleid follower of Meridia say he refuses to set foot in this city, that it was clearly the result of some dark ritual._ 

RALAKAI: There is a saying where I come from. “Naught without sacrifice.” The Great Trees are a Gift from the Green, say the prophets. For those who can truly understand the Will of the Spirit of the Now. The previous generations forgot this. That is all I will speak on that matter. Oh, and consider buying this bone-charm, taken from the neck of a hippogriff. You may need it if you slip from a tall tree.

**Thus concluded my interview. If you find yourself anywhere near Falinesti, consider visiting** _**The Top Branch**_. **The modestly priced slowfall charm saved my life immediately following the interview.**
