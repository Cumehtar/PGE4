**Background:**
The “[[Baandari Coast]]” is a name given to a region along the Southern Sea where piracy is highly common. The city-states of the region are all de facto independent, though as a result of outside imperialism between the late Mede Empire and the old Aldmeri Dominion and their successor states, pirates and smugglers have a large sway over the local economy. Regional governors encourage this situation in order to preserve their independence from outside political powers. In [[Senchal]], a “Corsair-King” rules outright. 

The Baandari Coast is inspired mostly by the Barbary Coast and “the Golden Age of Piracy” in the Caribbean, including the Flying Gang of Nassau. The Pirate Queen Shi Yang's influence in the South China Sea is another suggested source of inspiration.

**Society and Culture:**
Society on the Baandari Coast can be described as highly diverse and independent. Both in the sense of cultural diversity, as well as economic and intellectual diversity. The lax laws and regulations make it a prime boomtown for the soul industry; especially when combined with the fact that the Southern Sea in general sees much more traffic now that the Sea of Ghosts froze over. Khajiit are still the majority (in some rural regions, moon sugar is even an informal currency of sorts), but one can find people of every profession and background trying to find their fortune in the Baandari Coast. This also makes it a dangerous place: all the political powers would like to dominate the region, and so clashes between privateers and pirates as well as raids on merchant vessels are all too common.

On individual ships, crews often set elect the quatermaster and negotiate the rules for distribution of loot.
 
**Relationships with Others:**
As mentioned above, most of the other political powers would like to dominate the Southern Sea and the Baandari Coast. The city-states, for their part, would prefer to remain independent. The corsairs take advantage of this situation to make a profit, though some pirates are true “Baan Dar patriots” who believe in the freedom of the sea and the people of the Coast.

**Main Themes:**
The Baandari Coast explores the classic tropes of “pirate adventure” stories, chiefly the ideas about “freedom at sea” and “defiance of authority”. [[Yzmul gra-Maluk]] very much believes in the romantic ideal; the [[Cheydinhal Geographical Society]] argues the pirates and the Coast live up to a much more brutal reality. We leave it up to the reader’s imagination.
