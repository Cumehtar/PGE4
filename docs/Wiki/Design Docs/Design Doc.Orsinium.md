**Background:**
[[Orsinium]] is a reclusive theocracy living in the mountains between what was once Hammerfell and Skyrim. Its rulers, a group called the Beseechers, sail to Oblivion on whaleships where they claim to gain an audience with Malacath himself. Fiercely isolationist due to the long history of other nations invading and subjugating the Orcish people, Orsinium has built large underground tunnels and bunkers and repurposed much Dwemer tech. The very air is patrolled by airships and a Dragon mercenary ([[Nahfahlaar]], in fact).

Orsinium is influenced by Orcish lore from across the franchise, as well as traditional fantasy Dwarves and a bit of solarpunk.

**Society and Culture:**
On the surface (literally), Orsinium consists of one great eponymous city and some outlying settlements who subsist on pastoralism and agriculture. Underneath the great city is a vast network of tunnels and bunkers fed by Aetherial nets which make great use of mining and smithing to help support the Orsinium economy. As mentioned above, the entire polity is led by a group called the Beseechers who make regular trips to Oblivion and claim to speak for Malacath himself. Some of the Deep Orcs are abandoning Malacath worship however, becoming impious like their Dwemer forebears, and the Iron Orcs were never devout Malacathites to begin with.

In addition to Orcs, Ogres, and Goblins, it is worth noting that a substantial number of descendents of the Imperial Legions live in Orsinium, causing many residents to have Mannish and Merish ancestry.

**Relationships with Others:**
As devout isolationists, Orsinium does not have strong ties to any other country. They only allow traders to visit the surface-level of the city, for example. A notable exception is [[Greater Wrothgaria and Karth]], who Orsinium pays tribute to in order to maintain good public relations. Meanwhile Orsinium is rather annoyed with the frequent raids from the [[Bjoul]] tribes of the Great Steppe, and is paranoid that the [[Yokedate]] may one day try to sack their great city once more.

**Main Themes:**
Orsinium is a paranoid, isolationist, reclusive state. While this would clearly put them in a negative light from the [[Second Potentate]]’s point of view, [[Yzmul gra-Maluk]] has a strange affinity for the region as Orsinium is technically her ancestral homeland (her father left before she was born). This allows for much contrast in conflicting bias.
