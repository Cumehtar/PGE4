**Background:**
[[Bloodtoil]] is what remains of Valenwood. The Green Lady and the Silvenar, during the Second Great War, had a cosmic divorce due to a disagreement about her (seeming?) violation of the Green Pact when she went on the ultimate Wild Hunt against the Thalmor and then didn’t die afterward. One third the Bosmer followed the Silvenar north, to Kvatch, one third joined the Wildercourt of Greendshade, another third stayed with the Green Lady and became much more primal and animalistic in appearance. The region, too, became a sort of haven for beastfolk of all sorts due to being led by an Imga prophet resentful of how beastfolk were treated. The new dominant religion rejected all trappings of “civilization” as corrupting influence on the Green. Thus the majority of the region designated “Bloodtoil” is a loose tribal confederation.

Bloodtoil is influenced by descriptions of Valenwood in the original *Pocket Guide to the Empire* and T*he Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind*, as well as the *Morrowind* concept art for the Bosmer.

**Society and Culture:**
The society of Bloodtoil could be described as “anarcho-primitivist”, or even just “anarcho-Greenist”. Returning to “the Green” is treated as the paramount political, social, and spiritual goal of the leadership of most of the country after the intervention of foreign Dominions and Empires has seemed to only lead to disaster and ruin for the people of the Valenwood. As a “country”, Bloodtoil is rather isolationist, though this runs the gamut from merely wanting to be left alone to active hatred of all things “corrupted by the civilized world”. There are also old tribal grudges and internal cultural disputes between the differing groups which cause occasional skirmishes, though these are usually resolved at the “Chorus”, a meeting of representatives from each tribe, city, village, and clan, moderated by a High Prophet of the Green.
As for religion, there is of course much diversity throughout the region, but the most dominant faith is the “Green Prophecy”. It is an animistic faith which is fixated on trying to get in contact with “the Green” (Nature), the ever-shifting Spirit of the Now. They do not deny the existence of various deities, but rather interpret them as various aspects of the Green. There are spirits they may reject as not being aspects of the Green, which can lead to theological debate.

**Relationships with Others:**
Bloodtoil’s greatest foe is the [[New Ayleid Imperium]] to the north, who has seized much of their Sacred Wood and probably looks to keep taking land in the future. They have strange allies in the Baandari Corsairs and the Mother-Navigators, due to their common interest in defying more centralized states. The [[Second Potentate]] is horrified by the Bloodtoil Pack’s lack of a proper central government with normal laws and trade regulations. The Camorans of the [[Freehold Republic]] are rather perturbed by the wild Bloodtoil Pack so near their city of Woodhearth.

**Main Themes:**
The Bloodtoil Pack is an attempt to restore some of the older concepts about the Bosmer, that they were borderline beast-like or fae-like creatures more attuned to nature, not just “Forest Elves”. Bloodtoil also gives us a chance to explore other Beastfolk cultures, and how they, too, are not so different from Men and Mer. Or, explore the ways in which they are different.
