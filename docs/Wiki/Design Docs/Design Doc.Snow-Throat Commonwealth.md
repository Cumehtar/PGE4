**Background:**
War, plague, vampires, and dragons devastated Skyrim and isolated the province from the rest of Tamriel. Each region (“Hold”) of Skyrim basically split off from each other, and by the time some semblance of order re-emerged Western Skyrim united with parts of High Rock (see [[Design Doc.Wrothgaria and Karth]]) while Eastern Skyrim returned to the rule of moots, becoming a heavily decentralized semi-democracy known as the [[Snow-Throat Commonwealth]].

The Snow-Throat Commonwealth is inspired partially by the Icelandic Commonwealth, partially by the early United States, and a bit by the *Malazan* series by Steven Erikson.

**Society and Culture:**
Each Hold has its own way of forming government. E.g., [[Whiterun]] elects a Jarl every other year from their citizens, while the Jarl of the [[Aalto]] is a High Priestess of Kyne. Each Hold sends representatives to the Great Moot at [[Ivarstead]], who pass national legislation and elect a “High King” (who can be removed at any time by a majority vote). Wandering “[[Dragon Monks]]” trained in the Way of the Voice act as independent judges in civil and criminal cases.

Technologically, Snow-Throat appears quite backwards to foreigners. They have not taken part yet in the great soul-powered industrialism, and prefer old-fashioned approaches to magic. However, they *are* innovating with technologies such as windmills and firearm-like crossbows.

Religiously, the people of Snow-Throat seem to practice an eclectic mix of religious customs. They worship the old Nordic gods at various temples, but the practice of “Neo-Ysmirism” is also on the rise, a monastic tradition with two competing strains about how the Way of the Voice should be practiced.

**Relationships with Others:**
The Commonwealth is most tense with the Kingdom of Wrothgaria and Karth, particularly over the territory of [[Dawnstar]]. The [[Second Potentate]] sees the Commonwealth as a bunch of rustic barbarians, though not much of a threat at this time. Ironically, despite ancient feuds between Nords and Dunmer, [[Resdayn]] has good trade relations with the Commonwealth at the moment.

**Main Themes:**
The narrative of Snow-Throat is that of a decentralized nation that has yet to fully define itself. Some of the Holds are very embracing of grassroots democracy, while others cling to aristocracy and theocracy. Further, the conflict over how to properly interpret the “Way of the Voice” among the Dragon Monks can be seen as a continuation of themes from *The Elder Scrolls V.*
