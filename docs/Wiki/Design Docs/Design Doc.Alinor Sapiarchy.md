**Background:**
To understand the [[Sapiarchy of Alinor]] one must first understand a little bit of Summerset history. According to the PGE3, Alinor formerly had a very stratified caste structure: “(a)t the top are the Wise, teachers and priests, followed by Artists, Princes, Warriors, Landowners, Merchants, and Workers. Below Workers were the beasts, such as the enslaved goblins, who the Aldmer used to perform the jobs beneath the dignity of the very least of them.” We can infer, based on the events of TESIII and the subtext of PGE3, that this system was upset by the arrival of the Septim Empire and its preference for mercantilism and rule by figure-head monarchs. Therefore the choice by the Thalmor in TESV’s backstory materials to overthrow the “rightful rulers” of the Isles can be read both as a nationalist uprising against “Imperial puppets” and an ultraconservative attempt to “restore the Natural Order”.

Of course, the Thalmor would attempt to wage war with the Heavens by shooting Talos down from Aetherius, and sacrifice an entire city in the process. This, combined with war and plague, led to a popular uprising and inevitable collapse. A more moderate faction of The Wise, aka the Sapiarchs, would reassert their power, but not before a number of Artist-led communes and Goblin enclaves had proliferated across the isle of Alinor, and Auridon would be lost to the Merchants.

In terms of influences, Alinor pulls a bit from the “philospher-king” idea of Plato’s *Republic*, Soviet-style technocracy, as well as Ted Peterson’s description of the Beautiful from the *Pocket Guide to the Empire, Third Edition*, namely that they were supposed to reflect the Chinese and French Revolutions.

**Society and Culture:**
Alinor is ostensibly a mageocracy, with the various Sapiarchs governing each district more-or-less independently of one another. In practice, these Sapiarchs are too focused on their personal pet projects and academic debate to do much actual governance, so mid-level bureaucrats run the major cities while the outer towns and villages are essentially self-governing communes. In the artist-led communes, there is a heavy emphasis on continual self-reinvention and revolutionizing of society. The Psijic Endeavor has returned to Veloth’s original homeland.

Speaking of religion, the Psijic Old Ways is the dominant religion throughout Alinor, owing in part to a backlash to the Thalmor’s zealous fervor. Most Alinori are fairly secular people in general, though some ancestor and Daedra worship has resurged.

**Relationships with Others:**
Alinor is the main trade partner with the [[Iliac League]], which the [[Second Potentate]] is slightly annoyed about. A militant element in the Sapiarchy would like control of the Auridon Strait from the [[Freehold Republic]].

**Main Themes:**
The main theme of the Sapiarchy is the great divide between the centrally-planned, technocratic world of the Sapiarchs in the major cities, and the more grassroots, experimental world of the Beautiful in the surrounding communes. This dichotomy can be explored for internal tension.
