**Background:**
The Reachfolk saw the Silver Plague which shook the rest of Tamriel as a test from their lord Peryite, and they passed it gloriously. Under the leadership of a war-leader named [[Melusa Swordclaw]], they reclaimed Markarth and most of the Eastern and Western Reach and established a new kingdom under the Ard (Reachfolk word for monarch).

The main inspirations are Reachfolk in TES V and ESO as well as Celtic history.
 
**Society and Culture:**
Reachfolk society is currently divided both within and without. Urban Reachfolk have somewhat accepted “Imperialized” ways and Aedric worship, while rural “Wilder” Reachfolk cling to nomadic hunting and the Old Gods (Daedra). Meanwhile, the [[Druadach Kingdom]] also has to integrate all the other groups living under the Ard’s banner: the Nords, Redguards, Bretons, and Orcs. One attempt to maintain unity designed by the royal vateshran (essentially, bard) is to promote a highly syncretic faith that argues a variety of gods are actually “guises” of the same primal spirits. So far, this has seen only mixed success.

It is also worth mentioning that the Druadach Kingdom is one of the few places in Tamriel where lycanthropy is not only tolerated, but at times even encouraged. Hagravens are also revered as elders.

**Relationships with Others:**
The Druadach Kingdom has mixed relations with essentially all its neighbors, but what else is new for the Reachfolk? They have formed alliances with some of the Falmer clans of Blackreach.

**Main Themes:**
The Druadach Kingdom essentially continues the narrative thread of the Forsworn storyline of TESV by asking what happens when the Reachfolk *do* get a nation of their own, and now have to maintain it in spite of internal and external cultural tension.
