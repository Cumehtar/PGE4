**Background:**
The [[Queen Elisif]] of [[Solitude]] and the Queen of [[Northpoint]] entered a marital alliance which would provide the foundation for a new state: [[Greater Wrothgaria and Karth]]. As a result of war, plague, and the Sea of Ghosts freezing over, GW&K reverted back to the rule of “thane-barons” who could provide grain and the defense of the land. In other words, GW&K is a “feudal medieval fantasy” realm in Tamriel. It also takes many inspirations from the Holy Roman Empire.

**Society and Culture:**
Society in Greater Wrothgaria and Karth is very feudal, with most people entangled in a series of vassalage contracts. The High Queen and/or King travels throughout the realm, making their presence known to local rulers and reminding them of their obligations.

As Castle Dour became the main theological center of the North due to its prestige and association with the royal family, the predominant religion of Greater Wrothgaria and Karth preaches the importance of the Eight Divines. Technically, it does not dispute the spiritual authority of the Archbishop of the One in Cyrodiil City, the Kingdom just prefers a more conservative theology. Outside the major cities, witchcraft and druidry are quite common; especially the further west on the map one travels. There is also a group of rebel werewolves called the [[Hörme]] who revere Potema Septim.
 
**Relationships with Others**:
Greater Wrothgaria and Karth get along the most poorly with its eastern neighbor, the [[Snow-Throat Commonwealth]]. They also have had historical feuds with the [[Druadach Kingdom]] over the city of [[Evermore]]. GW&K is liked by the [[Second Potentate]], who see it as a quaint tourist destination. Due to shared ancestry and feudal land claim rights, GW&K has some territorial disputes with the Iliac League. [[Orsinium]] gets along well with GW&K, due to tributary arrangements.

**Main Themes:**
The Kingdom of Greater Wrothgaria and Karth is an attempt to explain the “stereotypical medieval fantasy” vibes of TESII, TESIV, and TESV through ‘convergent evolution’ worldbuilding. There is also a strong theme of nostalgia in GW&K, emphasized by the royal family’s obsession with the Septim Dynasty (who they claim descent from via Andorak Septim) and upholding the Septim Doctrines in religious faith.
