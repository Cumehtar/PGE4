**Background:**
The [[Great Steppe]] is not actually a nation, but a region of Tamriel heavily disputed by many tribes, nations, and peoples, roughly stretching from the Bjoulsae River to the Great Forest in Colovia. Said to be filled with lost ancient treasures, mysterious cities, and countless tribes; all of which is probably being sensationalized in the usual TES “unreliable narrator” sort of way. Inspired by the Silk Road and old-timey adventure literature.
 
**Cultures:**
The current cultures we have written about for the Great Steppe include: 

The ever-raiding [[Bjoul]], aka the Bjoulsae horse-raiders, who are not a single group but actually a variety of mixed-race tribes only identified as sharing a common ancestry by outsiders due to their affiliation with Centaurs and their apparent descent from the Bjoulsae River peoples. At the time of the Guide’s writing, a Bjoul warlord named Pennethal seeks to unite the tribes into a singular nation.

The nomadic [[Mother-Navigators]], a syncretic Khajiiti-Redguard faction who use moon sugar to commune with Khenarthi-Tava and find the best moonlit paths through the wilderness. 

Tribes called the “[[Imperial Legions]]” who identify themselves by Legion banner and affiliation (even though the Old Empire is long gone). Martial and mostly nomadic. One can be adopted or recruited into a “Legion”, not just blood descend.

The [[Nu-Folk]], aka Mantikora, who live in [[Craglorn]]. Seem to be friendly with the Iron Orcs.

Note that many of the tribes are pitted against each other, or against the [[Yokedate]] and the [[Druadach Kingdom]], by outside mercenary companies.
