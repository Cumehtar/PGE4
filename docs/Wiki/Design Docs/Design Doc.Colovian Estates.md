**Background:**
It has been said in previous Guides that without a strong Emperor to unite Cyrodiil, Colovia always retreats into itself, keeping the national spirit alive until a worthy heir presents himself. The collapse of the Mede Empire would seem to keep with this mythology, as the [[Colovian Estates]] have splintered into autonomous city-states once more. While they are mostly united in their common culture and resistance to [[Bjoul]] raiders, they also frequently bicker with one another. As a result, the governments of the Colovian West have never fully recovered from the chaos of the plague and war, and have come to rely on the [[East Empire Company]] for security and other utilities.

Inspired by Renaissance Europe and the East India Company.

**Society and Culture:**
The cities of Colovia are still similar to how they were under the old Septim and Mede Empires: aristocracies with some rights for the middle class. This is especially true in [[Chorrol]], which considers itself the “true heir” of the Old Empire, and a worthy rival of [[Cheydinhal]]. (Cheydinhal likely disagrees with both assessments.) Also of note is the Minotaur kingdom of [[Sancre Tor]] and the high Goblin population in [[Sutch]]. Religiously, [[Neo-Alessianism]] is dominant, albeit in diverse forms.

Outside the cities, many nomadic tribes and bandit gangs roam freely. This has led to the region’s reputation as a “wildland,” not aided by the fact that most security is left to EEC mercs, and that [[Skingrad]] is ruled by an elderly vampire (Hassildor from TESIV).

**Relationships with Others:**
The Colovian Estates each vie with each other for power and influence over the region, but also try to stay united against their common enemies (Bjoulsae hordes, Neo-Ayleids, bandits). The Yokedate desires control over the Craglorn region, and [[Elinhir]]. There may be some interaction between the Goblins of Sutch and the Goblins seeking better rights in the [[Freehold Republic]].

**Main Themes:**
A main narrative of the Colovian Estates is the encroachment of the EEC into people’s lives, essentially acting as a privatized government, and the complacency of the Estates’ rulers in allowing them to do so. It is also a kind of “Wild West”, filled with lawlessness anywhere one steps outside the major cities.
