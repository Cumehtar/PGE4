**Background:**
For mysterious reasons, Thras turned into a giant pillar. So, a bunch of Sload appeared in Tamriel with cures for the Silver Plague that had been devastating the population of the continent. These Sload were, eventually, allowed to mostly assimilate into certain regions of Tamriel. Later, a much more militant group of Sload invaded the Argonian city of [[Lilmoth]] and renamed it “[[New Thras]].” The local Hist tree went rogue, and now reigns over the peninsula alongside the necromantic slug-barons. 

New Thras is inspired by the “industrialization of nature” from *The Lord of the Rings*, and the general idea of Sload having a “victory” in Tamrielic history.

**Society and Culture:**
New Thras is a reclusive and isolationist society, though it is seemingly a hot spot for necromancers and soul-traders across Tamriel. It is heavily industrialized, though in the sense that the undead have been turned into mechanized labor for the magical elite.

**Relationships with Others:**
New Thras is in a perpetual state of war with [[Argonia]], though at time of the Guide’s writing it amounts to little more than border skirmishes and raids. The Baandari corsairs likely do a lot of trading with New Thras.
 
**Main Themes:**
New Thras is hopefully a means of expanding the Sload, giving them more depth than what the *Elder Scrolls* franchise has shown them so far, and also exploring the effects necromancy would have on a societal scale.
